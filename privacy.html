Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Mero Karobar (“the app”) is a personal and business ledger for income, expenses, loans, EMI instalments, tasks and bank balances. It is provided by Milan Patel. Questions about this policy: pmilan677@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

  • Your Google profile (name, email address and profile picture), only to show who is signed in and to keep each person’s ledger separate.
  • Your Google Drive “app data” folder (the permission called drive.appdata). This is a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only this app can open. The app cannot see any of your other Drive files.
  • The information you type into the app, such as income, expenses, loans, payments, tasks and bank balances.

Where your data is stored

Your ledger is saved as small files in the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive, and a copy is kept in your browser on the device you use so the app works quickly and offline. The app runs in your browser and talks directly to Google. The developer does not receive, store or have access to your ledger or your Drive. There is no developer database.

How the data is used

Only to show your ledger to you, calculate balances and statements, and save and sync your ledger between your devices. The data is not sold, not shared with advertisers, not used for advertising or analytics, and not used to train machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Mero Karobar’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party services

  • Google provides sign-in and Google Drive.
  • Website hosting is provided by Vercel, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address) needed to deliver the website. These logs do not contain your ledger.
  • Code libraries used to create PDF and Excel downloads are loaded from the cdnjs content delivery network. Those requests contain no ledger data.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Open Help › Account › Delete all my data from Google Drive in the app to remove everything the app saved in your Drive.
  • Or, in Google Drive, open Settings › Manage apps, choose Mero Karobar and select Delete hidden app data.
  • You can remove the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Sign out removes the copy stored in your browser on that device.

Security

The app uses Google’s sign-in and sends data to Google over encrypted connections. Access tokens are kept in the browser’s memory and are not sent to the developer. Anyone who can use your unlocked device or Google account can see your ledger, so protect both.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
